Marketing Analytics & Campaign Executive

Titan Company Ltd · Bangalore, India
2022 – 2024

One measurement view for 11 brands

Challenge: brand teams each requested their own reports, so performance figures took days to arrive and were hard to compare.

What I did: built self-serve dashboards in Salesforce Marketing Cloud covering engagement, content ROI and SLA performance.

Reporting turnaround fell from several days to under one day.

Finding which campaigns paid back

Challenge: budget was spread across email, social, web and in-store with little evidence of what earned its spend.

What I did: measured campaign performance by audience segment and objective, comparing formats and brand voices against spend.

Clear read-outs that helped shift budget and effort towards what converted.

Decisions based on customer behaviour

Challenge: campaign planning leaned on instinct.

What I did: designed tests and analysed drop-off points, recurring query themes and peak-hour patterns, alongside competitor research.

Campaign decisions moved from assumption-led to evidence-led.

Peak season across 11 brand voices

Challenge: Diwali and Akshaya Tritiya drive most Indian jewellery sales, and every brand launches at once.

What I did: owned tone of voice and content calendars for all 11 brands, managing approvals through Jira.

Quality held across 10,000+ daily customer interactions at peak.

An early move into AI customer service

Challenge: a GPT-based customer communications platform had to launch safely, about a year ahead of wider industry adoption.

What I did: wrote tone guidelines for AI replies, ran user acceptance testing and documented escalation rules.

The platform went live with on-brand replies and clear hand-offs to people.

Working with every team

Challenge: data only helps if non-analysts can act on it.

What I did: partnered with marketing, sales and client-facing teams, turning campaign and customer data into plain recommendations.

Read-outs that people outside analytics used to make decisions.

Day to day, I was also responsible for

  • Producing weekly and monthly performance read-outs for brand managers and leadership
  • Agreeing KPIs and reporting formats with each brand team, so results were measured the same way across all 11 brands
  • Running post-campaign reviews after major launches to capture what to repeat and what to drop
  • Tracking competitor campaigns and offers, especially in the run-up to festive seasons
  • Coordinating content calendars and approvals with creative, sales and client-facing teams during simultaneous launches
  • Monitoring SLA performance for customer communications and flagging issues before they reached customers
  • Showing colleagues how to use the self-serve dashboards so they could find answers without waiting for a report
  • Writing and maintaining tone of voice guidelines for each brand

Titan's figures and dashboards are confidential, so these summaries describe the work rather than show internal data.

Online Assistant, Groceries Online

Sainsbury's · London · part-time
2026 – present

I pick and prepare online grocery orders in a busy London store. It's fast, target-driven work, and it gives me a close-up view of how ecommerce works for a major UK retailer.

Working to targets

Every shift is measured on per-order time, pick rate and accuracy. I consistently meet Sainsbury's performance benchmarks, including at the busiest times of the week.

Thinking about the customer

When an item is out of stock, I choose the best substitute in real time, balancing what's on the shelf against what the customer is likely to want.

Live systems

I use Sainsbury's handheld systems for live order, stock and dispatch management, keeping accuracy high across high-volume shifts.

What it's taught me

Product availability, substitutions and delivery all shape how customers feel about a brand. Seeing UK grocery ecommerce from the inside helps me understand the operations behind customer data.
